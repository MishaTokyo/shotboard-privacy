Shotboard Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

In short: Shotboard does not collect, transmit or sell any of your data. Everything you create stays on your Mac.

Data we collect

None. Shotboard has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash-reporting services. The app does not connect to any server operated by us or by third parties.

Your projects and images

Projects, text and reference images you add are stored locally on your Mac, inside the app's own storage folder, together with automatic version history. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it.

Sharing and export

When you use Share to export a PDF or a .shotboard project file, the file is saved to the location you pick. What you do with that file afterwards — for example sending it to someone — happens outside Shotboard and is up to you.

Clipboard and files

Shotboard reads the clipboard only when you paste into the app, and opens only the files you choose or drop into it. Nothing is read in the background.

Children

Shotboard does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact the developer, Mykhailo Sotnikov, through the support link on the Shotboard App Store page.
